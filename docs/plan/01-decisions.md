# Decisions

Each entry is a decision that could reasonably have gone the other way, recorded as
**Why.** / **The cost.** — because *a decision recorded without its cost is advocacy rather
than a record*, and advocacy is no use to the person who has to change it later.

Decisions inherited from demo-kit are not repeated here. This file holds the ones this demo
made for itself: its schema, its detectors, its prompts, its tour copy, its palette. Those
are the work a demo is.
