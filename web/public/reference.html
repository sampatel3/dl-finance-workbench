The Problem

The model is the easy half. Production is where it stalls.

An enterprise can generate almost anything now. What it usually cannot do is answer the five questions its own control environment asks of anything that touches operations — is it integrated, evaluated, controlled, observable, operable? Most AI work has no answer, so most AI work stays a demo.

The pilot that impresses a steering committee and cannot survive an auditor asking how it decided.
The proof of concept that never touched a production system, so a year of it proves nothing about operating one.
The vendor who scoped the work, handed it over a wall, and was gone by the time it had to run on a Tuesday.

How It Works

How the work is engaged, and how it is delivered — two models, and they are not the same model.

How It Works

Four functions own the account, and a gate stands in front of the build money

Fixed-scope work runs through the Quartet. The point of it is that nobody hands your programme to a delivery team who did not scope it, and nobody spends build budget before the evidence is in.

The Quartet. Data, Delivery, Engineering and AI, each led by an executive director who stays on your account from the first conversation through go-live. Squads cascade under the four and new hires own their own clients, so the structure grows without the accountability moving.
Discovery answers which problem, before you commit to how. Where the roadmap does not exist yet, an eight-week Quartet Discovery produces the AI strategy, the prioritised use-case roadmap and the governance framework — and you can stop there.
The gate is at week three, and it is real. On a twelve-week Accelerate, if the data, the value or the delivery path is not viable, the work stops and you pay for discovery alone — keeping the data-quality assessment, the baseline and the scope whoever builds it.
Your risk function is a first-class output. Model documentation, evaluation evidence, control mapping, monitoring and alerting, and the operating runbook — produced by the build as it happens rather than written up after it.

Roadmap

Twelve weeks to live, and you can leave at week three

Evidence before build, operation before handover. Every phase ends in something you keep whether or not the next one happens.

01 / Weeks 1–3
Discovery and data audit

The workflow, its value, its baseline, its data, the systems, the controls and who owns it. Ends in a go or a no-go — and a no-go bills discovery alone.

02 / Weeks 4–8
Build and evaluate

The product, validated on your data against the measures agreed at the gate. You get back-test evidence and an accuracy comparison, not a demonstration.

03 / Weeks 9–11
Integrate and deploy

Production systems, permissions, controls, monitoring and the people who use it. A deployment and a runbook rather than a pilot.

04 / Week 12
Validate and hand over

Live performance against the week-one baseline, knowledge transferred, ownership named, and the roadmap for what follows.

Transformation runs a longer shape: the eight-week Quartet Discovery, then AI Discovery for a prioritised roadmap with quantified business cases, then an innovation portfolio validated for production, then adoption and governance.

No cloud platform in September. Live on the national platform in January.

Jan 2025

Platform live, and with it the first Open Finance data-sharing APIs in the UAE. Four months from nothing to serving.

Feb 2025

First live transaction across the national platform, answering in 100–150ms against the regulator’s 500ms ceiling.

May 2025

First CBUAE certification, and the first participant to clear all three go-live certifications.

Bounded precisely: we built and instrumented the bank’s serving estate and helped shape the reporting-data requirements. We did not build Nebras, Ozone or Al Tareq. The Ask

Bring one problem and thirty minutes. Leave with a written verdict.

Three questions, one call. If Accelerate is not the right route we say so — and if the answer is that you are not ready for any of it, that is a useful thing to have been told in half an hour.

What we ask you

Value: what outcome matters, who owns it, and how it is measured today. Readiness: whether the workflow, data, systems, controls and ownership can carry a production product. Route: Accelerate, Transformation, or our people inside yours.

What you leave with

A fit verdict, a recommended route, a first scope, a success measure and a next step — in writing, and yours whether or not anything follows.

Where to send it

If it is a fit, discovery starts and the gate is three weeks later.
