Accelerate · 12 Weeks

Accelerate takes the chosen scope live in twelve weeks

Discovery establishes the priority, value, data route and governance plan. Accelerate starts with a three-week readiness gate, then builds, evaluates, integrates and transfers the live product with its operating runbook.

Weeks 1–3
Readiness gate

Confirm the workflow, value, baseline, named owner, approved data access, security route and source interfaces. Proceed, rescope or stop.

Weeks 4–8
Build and evaluate

Build and validate on your data against measures agreed up front. Back-test the evidence and compare accuracy — not a demonstration.

Weeks 9–11
Integrate

Integrate with production systems, permissions, controls, monitoring and users. Deploy with a runbook rather than as a pilot.

Week 12
Operate

Measure live performance against the week-one baseline. Transfer knowledge, name ownership and set the roadmap for what follows.
