Roadmap

AI-native engineering, on our work and on yours

Drafting is cheap now, so the scarce skill is verification. Every engagement is run by people using agents to do the generation and owning what ships — and we run the same way internally, from qualification through to scoping.

Weeks 1–3
Evidence

The workflow, its value, its baseline, its data, the controls and who owns it. You keep the data-quality assessment and the baseline whatever happens next.

Weeks 4–8
Build and evaluate

Built and validated on your data against measures agreed up front. Back-test evidence and an accuracy comparison, not a demonstration.

Weeks 9–11
Integrate

Production systems, permissions, controls, monitoring and the people who use it. A deployment and a runbook rather than a pilot.

Week 12
Operate

Live performance against the week-one baseline, knowledge transferred, ownership named, and the roadmap for what follows.

Your risk function is a first-class output: model documentation, evaluation evidence, control mapping, monitoring and alerting, and the operating runbook — produced by the build as it happens rather than written up afterwards. Larger programmes run the same shape over a longer arc.
