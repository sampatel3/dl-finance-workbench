The Problem

The model is the easy half. Production is where it stalls.

Roadmap

Twelve weeks to live, and you can leave at week three

Evidence before build, operation before handover. Every phase ends in something you keep whether or not the next one happens.

01 / Weeks 1–3
Discovery and data audit

The workflow, its value, its baseline, its data, the systems, the controls and who owns it. Ends in a go or a no-go — and a no-go bills discovery alone.

02 / Weeks 4–8
Build and evaluate

The product, validated on your data against the measures agreed at the gate. Back-test evidence and an accuracy comparison, not a demonstration.

03 / Weeks 9–11
Integrate and deploy

Production systems, permissions, controls, monitoring and the people who use it. A deployment and a runbook rather than a pilot.

04 / Week 12
Validate and hand over

Live performance against the week-one baseline, knowledge transferred, ownership named, and the roadmap for what follows.

Your risk function is a first-class output: model documentation, evaluation evidence, control mapping, monitoring and alerting, and the operating runbook — produced by the build as it happens rather than written up after it. Transformation runs a longer shape, opening with the eight-week Quartet Discovery.

The Risks

Our record proves the method, not that it will work on your data

Everything two slides back happened somewhere else, on somebody else’s systems, with their owners. The gate exists so that finding out whether it holds for you costs three weeks rather than twelve.

The method
Evidenced

Seven products in live operation across two UAE institutions, under CBUAE, ADGM and FSRA, with controls and monitoring in production.

Your workflow
Proves on yours

Whether the data, the value and the delivery path hold for your workflow is what weeks one to three test — on your systems, not a reference case.

The gate
Week 3

A hard go/no-go. If data, value or the delivery path is not viable, the work stops — said to your face, in writing, with the reasons.

If it stops
Discovery only

A no-go bills discovery and nothing else. The data-quality assessment, baseline and product thesis are yours either way.
