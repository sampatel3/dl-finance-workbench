Case Study 01 · Banking
Traction + Proof

ADCB: data, AI and bank-wide resilience

Tier-1 UAE bank. Starting with no unified data platform and twenty-plus legacy integrations, Deeplight built an AWS data foundation and Azure AI platform, delivered four production products, and supported a four-phase bank-wide resilience programme designed to keep priority banking services operating through disruption.

Data foundation
60 days

Cloud-native AWS lakehouse with governed batch and real-time streaming, API management and self-service analytics. Processing fell from 72 hours to 15 minutes; data quality rose from 67% to 95%.

AI platform
Azure AI

Azure AI platform with governed streams, CDP and bi-directional CRM integration, data products and multiple AI/ML use cases — including enterprise-knowledge and mobile-banking chatbots.

Open Finance
First certified in UAE

From no cloud platform in September 2024 to platform and first APIs live in January 2025. First bank certified by CBUAE; 100–150ms typical response against a 500ms ceiling.

Bank-wide service continuity
4 phases · 5 RTO tiers

The Minimum Viable Bank identifies priority services and sequences recovery from always-on to <4 hours and longer tiers. Phase 3 is defining the target architecture; Phase 4 targets portable onshore/offshore recovery with regular live failover testing.

Platform outcomes

Two greenfield platforms → 4 products live. More than 2,000 Power BI users, 30% cost reduction and zero regulatory violations after implementation.

Case Study 02 · Energy
Traction + Proof

EWEC: data + AI platform.
Three products live.

UAE national energy buyer. Deeplight built the Databricks foundation and the MLOps lifecycle together. Governed pipelines, reusable features, controlled deployment and live monitoring support load forecasting, solar forecasting and reserve management as one production estate.

Data + AI platform
1 governed foundation

Operational, weather, historical and reference data land in a Databricks lakehouse with quality gates, lineage and reusable feature tables.

Products in operation
3 products live

Load forecasting, solar forecasting and reserve management run on the same governed platform rather than as separate one-off pipelines.

MLOps lifecycle
Versioned + controlled

MLflow tracks experiments and model versions; scheduled workflows run training and inference; controlled promotion and rollback move changes into production.

Monitor + improve
Drift-triggered

Accuracy, data quality and drift are monitored by bucket. Champion/challenger comparison and threshold-triggered retraining keep models current while operators retain the decision.

Production estate

One governed lifecycle from ingestion to intervention. Data engineering, AI engineering, product and operational adoption remained connected through go-live.

Accelerate · 12 Weeks

Accelerate takes the chosen scope live in twelve weeks

Discovery establishes the priority, value, data route and governance plan. Accelerate starts with a three-week readiness gate, then builds, evaluates, integrates and transfers the live product with its operating runbook.

Weeks 1–3
Readiness gate

Confirm the workflow, value, baseline, named owner, approved data access, security route and source interfaces. Proceed, rescope or stop.

Weeks 4–8
Build and evaluate

Build and validate on your data against measures agreed up front. Back-test the evidence and compare accuracy — not a demonstration.

Weeks 9–11
Integrate

Integrate with production systems, permissions, controls, monitoring and users. Deploy with a runbook rather than as a pilot.

Week 12
Operate

Measure live performance against the week-one baseline. Transfer knowledge, name ownership and set the roadmap for what follows.
