Appendix · EWEC Forecasting

Three forecasting products. One governed MLOps lifecycle.

EWEC, the UAE’s national energy buyer. Load forecasting, solar forecasting and reserve management operate on one Databricks data-and-AI platform. The shared estate controls what models can see, how changes reach production, and what happens when accuracy or data quality moves.

Product 01

Load forecasting

Demand outlook

Direct multi-horizon estimators use only information observable at forecast origin. Separate 5-, 15- and 60-minute inference and back-test workflows avoid feeding one prediction into the next horizon.

Product 02

Solar forecasting

Generation outlook

Shared feature tables combine time encodings, PV lags, rolling statistics, irradiance persistence, cloud and clear-sky signals. Dropout repair happens before 15- and 60-minute aggregation.

Product 03

Reserve management

Operational planning support

Runs on the same governed inputs, product controls and monitoring estate as the forecasts. Operators keep the decision while the platform preserves the evidence behind each run.

01Ingest + test

Operational, weather, historical and reference data; quarantine at quality gates.

02Build features

Reusable, lineage-tracked tables with leak-proof origin and target-time separation.

03Train + back-test

Scheduled workflows compare performance by product, horizon and bucket.

04Register

MLflow records experiments, parameters, artefacts and model versions.

05Promote + roll back

Controlled Dev → Test → Prod release with registry and rollback.

06Monitor + improve

Accuracy, data quality, SLA and drift; champion/challenger and retraining triggers.

Known limits

Weather beyond the operational forecast horizon uses a historical seasonal profile, and the current estate uses one weather proxy across zones. Both limits are explicit, monitored and scoped for improvement; invalid outputs are logged rather than hidden.
