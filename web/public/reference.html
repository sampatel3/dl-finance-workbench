Roadmap

A twelve-week build with a real readiness gate

The first three weeks test the dependencies a schedule cannot wish away. At the gate we proceed, rescope or stop; throughout the build, evaluation evidence, control mapping, monitoring and the operating runbook are produced as part of the work.

Weeks 1–3
Readiness gate

Confirm the workflow, value, baseline, named owner, approved data access, security route and source interfaces. Then proceed, rescope or stop.

Weeks 4–8
Build and evaluate

Built and validated on your data against measures agreed up front. Back-test evidence and an accuracy comparison, not a demonstration.

Weeks 9–11
Integrate

Production systems, permissions, controls, monitoring and the people who use it. A deployment and a runbook rather than a pilot.

Week 12
Operate

Live performance against the week-one baseline, knowledge transferred, ownership named, and the roadmap for what follows.
