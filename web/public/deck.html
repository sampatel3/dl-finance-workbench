Roadmap

A twelve-week build from your ledgers to a real close.

The first three weeks test the dependencies a schedule cannot wish away. At the gate we proceed, rescope or stop; each stage leaves behind a usable artefact, and the workbench reads your ledgers but never writes to them.

Weeks 1–3
Readiness gate

Confirm one close, its comparator, mappings, owners, approved data access, security route, tenant or jurisdiction and source interfaces. You keep the data-quality assessment and week-one baseline.

Weeks 4–8
Build and evaluate

Reproduce a close your controllers already signed. Back-test the position, drivers and evidence on your data; name where it matches and where it does not.

Weeks 9–11
Integrate

Connect ERP and consolidation, align permissions to entity ownership, monitor pipelines, train controllers, and deliver controls, model documentation, evaluation evidence and the runbook.

Week 12
Operate

Run one real month-end through the workbench. Measure it against the baseline, name production ownership and scope the surfaces that follow.

Measured in week one and week twelve: working days to a signed close · restatements after sign-off · hours rebuilding the model, pack and commentary. The threshold is agreed with you in week one because this is Deeplight’s first Finance Workbench close on a client’s ledgers.
